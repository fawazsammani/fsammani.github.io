Fawaz Sammani

I'm a 3rd year PhD student at the ETRO department at Vrije Universiteit Brussel (VUB), supervised by Prof. Nikos Deligiannis, under the FWO Fellowship Grant. I was also a visiting researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Informatics in Saarbrücken, Germany supervised by Dr. Jonas Fischer. I did my Masters degree in Applied Computer Science at the VUB under the VUB Masters Scholarship Programme. My research interests focus on vision-language, zero-shot learning, generative models and inference-time approaches.
