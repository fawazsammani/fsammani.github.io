Fawaz Sammani

I'm a final year PhD student at the ETRO department at Vrije Universiteit Brussel (VUB) under the FWO Fellowship Grant. I was also a visiting researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Informatics in Saarbrücken, Germany supervised by Dr. Jonas Fischer. I did my Masters degree in Applied Computer Science at the VUB under the VUB Masters Scholarship Programme. My research interests focus on vision-language, explainable AI, zero-shot learning, unsupervised learning, generative models and inference-time approaches.
